# Summary of Findings

## 1. Order and Sales Analysis
- Delivered orders make up the largest share, suggesting strong fulfillment performance.
- Total Revenue: ₹71,257.12. This figure represents the confirmed revenue from orders 
that have successfully reached customers
- consistent order activity each month across 5+ years.
The dataset shows a growing trend in total revenue and volume over time..

## 2. Customer Analysis
- 33 customers have placed more than one order.Among these:4 customers placed 3 orders,29 customers placed 2 orders.
- Moderate fluctuation in customer engagement, with a typical range between 9–15 active customers per month.
Dec 2023 saw the highest number of active customers at 18 and Feb 2024 had the lowest with just 4 active customers.
- First-Time Orders: 698 orders (≈ 95%) came from new customers placing their first order.
Repeat Orders: Only 37 orders (≈ 5%) came from returning customers.

## 3. Payment Status Analysis
- Only 216 out of 664 payments (~33%) were successfully completed.
A high number of failed (224) and pending (224) payments is a red flag—this makes up 67% of all payment attempts.
- Failed payments correlated with specific months like dec2023, possibly due to technical or policy issues.
- Majority of payments were made via paypal[75 (completed) + 76 (failed) + 82 (pending)], while other methods were less used.

## 4. Customer Retention Analysis (Power BI)
- Out of 698 total customers, the majority are first-time customers .
- Months like June and March show relatively better retention in following months.
- Visualization revealed opportunities to improve long-term retention by re-engaging drop-off customers.

## Recommendations
- Focus on re-engagement campaigns for customers who stop ordering after the first month.
- Investigate reasons for failed payments and streamline the payment process.
- Leverage successful acquisition months to design future marketing strategies.
